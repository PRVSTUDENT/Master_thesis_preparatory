The controlled 2-cycle comparison of Abaqus built-in cyclic plasticity models showed a clear hierarchy in response level. Linear kinematic hardening produced the lowest force response and a stable symmetric loop, multilinear kinematic hardening gave an intermediate response, and the tuned combined-hardening model produced the strongest and widest loop. Because the geometry, mesh, loading history, and extraction procedure were kept unchanged, this trend can be attributed directly to the different hardening-law formulations. Therefore, for the current thesis stage, combined hardening is the most suitable built-in model for deeper study, while linear kinematic and multilinear kinematic remain useful baseline and intermediate comparison models.
